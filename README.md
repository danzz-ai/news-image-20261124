A classic broadsheet front page summing up 24 November 2026: the lead story is
the mine strikes on three oil tankers in the Strait of Hormuz, with a drawn
tanker-and-mine illustration, followed by six panels covering the Shenzhou-24
launch, fourteen nations backing the ICJ Rohingya ruling, North Korea expelling
the Swiss envoy, the Solomon Islands choosing Australia as security partner,
US tariff threats against China and the EU, and the day's market rally, plus an
"Also Today" ticker of four more stories. The page is rendered deterministically
from a Python + PIL script, so re-running it produces a byte-identical image.
